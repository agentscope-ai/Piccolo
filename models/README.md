# Models

Piccolo-4B, Piccolo-9B and Piccolo-27B are available as Safetensors and BF16, Q8_0 and Q4_K_M GGUF weights. These 12 foundation and GGUF repositories are published on Hugging Face and ModelScope under Apache-2.0. The [Piccolo-0.8B-VL](https://huggingface.co/agentscope-ai/Piccolo-0.8B-VL), [Piccolo-2B-VL](https://huggingface.co/agentscope-ai/Piccolo-2B-VL), [Piccolo-4B-VL](https://huggingface.co/agentscope-ai/Piccolo-4B-VL), [Piccolo-9B-VL](https://huggingface.co/agentscope-ai/Piccolo-9B-VL), and [Piccolo-27B-VL](https://huggingface.co/agentscope-ai/Piccolo-27B-VL) checkpoints are also available on Hugging Face.
