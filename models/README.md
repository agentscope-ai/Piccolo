# Models

Piccolo-4B, Piccolo-9B and Piccolo-27B are available as Safetensors and BF16, Q8_0 and Q4_K_M GGUF weights. These 12 foundation and GGUF repositories are published on Hugging Face and ModelScope under Apache-2.0. The 4B, 9B and 27B VL checkpoints are also available on Hugging Face.

- [Download matrix and inference requirements](../docs/MODELS.md)
- [Weight revisions, sizes and SHA-256 hashes](manifest.json)
- [Benchmark results and reproduction](../evaluation/README.md)
- [Reference inference](../inference/README.md)
- [QwenPaw setup](../docs/QWENPAW.md)

The [ModelScope collection](https://www.modelscope.cn/collections/AgentScope/Piccolo) includes the full checkpoints and every GGUF variant.
